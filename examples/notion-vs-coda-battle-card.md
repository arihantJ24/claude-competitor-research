## Assessment

**Notion strengths**
- Scale and default status: 13,871 G2 reviews vs 493, >$600M ARR disclosed, cash-flow positive (S10, S13, S4).
- Agent momentum: Custom Agents GA with >1M built in beta and named adopters with hard numbers (Remote: >95% triage accuracy) (S13).
- Lower like-for-like price per paid seat at both tiers: $10 vs $12, $20 vs $33 (S1 vs S5/S7/S11).
- SSO at Business tier, where Coda gates it to Enterprise (S1, S5).
- Pricing stability: Plus/Business list prices unchanged in the window; Coda's rose 33–50% on 8 Jul 2026 (S4, S7).

**Notion weaknesses**
- Every member is a paid seat; a 20-person team with 5 builders pays for 20 (S1).
- AI is now metered: Business AI moved from unlimited to an allowance (Aug 2026) and agents burn credits at $10 / 1,000 — a budgeting question reps can plant (S1, S4).
- Review themes are durable: learning curve 23%, performance on large databases 13%, offline/mobile 10% (S9); fresh 2026 quotes confirm (S15).
- Native automation and formulas are thinner than Coda's; third-party comparison says Coda's in-doc automations "can replace Zapier for many in-doc workflows" (S17).
- Product churn signal: Notion Mail launched Apr 2025, killed Sep 2026 (S12).

**Threat level to Coda: High.** Single biggest reason: Notion is cheaper per paid seat at every like-for-like tier *and* just shipped the category's most visible AI feature, at the exact moment Coda raised prices 33–50% and moved new buyers onto a suite bundle that currently cannot buy Packs (S1, S6, S7, S13).

**Three things to watch**
1. Whether Notion's AI allowance on Business tightens further (Aug 2026 change) — every tightening is a Coda talking point on predictable cost (S4).
2. Whether Superhuman restores Packs for new/bundle Docs customers; until then the integration story is weaker for new logos than for legacy ones (S6).
3. Notion Developer Platform / Workers adoption (May 2026) — if Workers mature, Notion closes the automation gap that is Coda's clearest product edge (S12).

**So what for us (this quarter):** Decide whether Superhuman Docs sells on *total cost at realistic maker ratios* or on *suite value* — the two stories conflict in a room. The public numbers support the first: at $33 / Doc Maker vs $20 / member, Coda is cheaper whenever fewer than ~61% of workspace members are makers (calc from S1 and S7; see Pricing in the room). That argument only works if the rep can quote a confirmed Doc Maker definition and the current Packs status, so the decision is to publish an internal one-pager on both before year-end renewals, and to stop leading with "cheaper" in any team where most members build.

## Battle card

**One-liner on them:** "The all-in-one AI workspace — docs, wikis, projects and agents in one place, already used by your team."
**One-liner for us:** "Notion is a great wiki that charges you for every reader. Coda is where ops teams build the tools they actually run on, and only the builders pay — now with Grammarly and Superhuman Go in the box."

**Why we win**
1. **Price per paid seat is the wrong metric; price per workspace is ours.** Only Doc Makers pay; Notion bills every member (S1, S5). For a 20-person team with 5 builders: Notion Business ≈ $400/month vs Docs Business ≈ $165/month (calc, yearly billing, Oct-26).
2. **Automation without Zapier.** Buttons and native automations are core to Coda; a third-party head-to-head says Notion "relies on integrations such as Zapier, Make" (S17 · May-26). Ties to Notion's 23% learning-curve / setup complaint share (S9).
3. **Formula depth.** Coda tables are "closer to Excel than to Notion's properties" (S17). Target ops and analytics buyers who hit Notion's property/rollup ceiling.
4. **Support quality.** G2 quality-of-support 9.1 vs Notion's 8.4 (S8, S10 · Aug-26).
5. **Predictable AI cost.** Notion Business AI became a usage allowance in Aug 2026 with credits at $10 / 1,000 (S1, S4). Ask what their last credit bill was.

**Why we lose**
1. **Default and familiarity.** 13,871 G2 reviews vs 493; many prospects' employees already use Notion personally (S10, S8).
2. **Agents.** Custom Agents are GA with >1M built and named enterprise proof (Ramp, Vercel, Remote) (S13). Docs has AI "in beta" (S11) and no equivalent agent story outside the suite's Superhuman Go.
3. **SSO one tier earlier.** Notion Business has SAML SSO at $20; Coda gates SSO to Enterprise (S1, S5). Mid-market IT will notice.
4. **Our own July price rise and rebrand.** Pro +50%, Team/Business +33% on list (S7); prospects who priced us in H1 2026 will feel it, and "Coda is now Superhuman Docs" costs a minute of every call.
5. **Packs gap for new buyers.** New Superhuman Docs / bundle customers cannot buy Packs "at this time" (S6). If the prospect needs a specific Pack, do not promise it.
6. **Performance at scale is a shared weakness, not an edge.** Coda has its own "10 seconds plus to open our main doc" quotes (S14). Do not attack Notion on speed.

**Landmines (questions to plant)**
- "How many of your members actually build pages versus read them — and are you paying Notion for all of them?"
- "What did your Notion credit usage look like last month, and who approves overage?" (S1, S4)
- "How many Zapier or Make zaps are keeping your Notion workflows alive, and who maintains them?" (S17)
- "What happens to your Notion Mail setup after 22 September?" (S12) — use only if they adopted it.
- "When your database passed a few thousand rows, what happened to load times?" (S9, S15) — plant only if the prospect's use case is small enough that Coda will not hit the same wall.
